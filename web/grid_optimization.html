Raw grid-cell output, not a bias-corrected forecast. Coordinates and capacities are the real mapped sites from OpenStreetMap, but a 0.1° (~10 km) cell averages over terrain and understates ridge-top hub-height wind — exactly where wind farms get built. The spread across the fleet is wide for that reason: coastal and plains sites produce, sites behind ridgelines read low. Rank and compare these numbers; bias-correct per site against SCADA history before treating any of them as expected production. Solar is GHI-driven and needs no such correction.
